# NikNote Sales Playbook

A working script for getting the first paying customers. Not a strategy document.
Read the section you need, then go send the messages.

---

## Rule 0 — Sell only what exists

Sell this, because it works today, in the browser, offline, with no AI key:

- Type or paste text → realistic handwritten pages
- 16+ handwriting styles
- 300 DPI print-ready PDF export
- PDF merge / split / compress / e-sign
- OCR: photograph a page of notes, get editable text
- Works offline once installed

**Do not sell:** AI generation, teacher dashboard, student analytics, bulk
import, white-label, SSO, LMS integration. None of it exists yet. If someone
asks, say "not built yet, I'll tell you when it is." That answer has closed more
deals than a lie ever will.

## Priority 1 — Tuition centres and coaching institutes (₹4,999/year)

This is the fastest path to real money. One sale = ~50 consumer subscriptions.
There are lakhs of them, they answer WhatsApp, and the buyer is one person who
can say yes on the spot.

### Who exactly

Not Kota factories. Start with **small and mid-size, 50–500 students**, within
driving distance of you:

- Neighbourhood tuition centres (Class 6–12, Maths/Science/English)
- CBSE/ICSE coaching centres
- Typing/test-prep institutes
- Local branches of national chains (they have a local centre head)
- Small colleges (B.Ed, B.Com, polytechnic) — assignment-heavy courses

### How to build the list (free, 2 hours)

1. Google Maps → search "coaching classes near me", "tuition centre <your city>",
   "CBSE coaching <area>". Note name, phone, owner name, student count guess.
2. Instagram/Facebook → search "coaching <your city>". Many have pages with
   WhatsApp numbers.
3. Walk in. 20 centres in a day. The conversion rate on a face-to-face visit is
   higher than any cold email.
4. Referrals: after any conversation, ask "aap kis aur institute ko jaante ho
   jinko yeh useful lagega?"

Target: **100 names in the list before you send a single message.**

### The pitch — the actual problem

A teacher making a worksheet or test paper by hand spends 30–40 minutes copying
it out. Multiply by 3 worksheets a week × 40 weeks = **~76 hours a year**, per
teacher, spent copying.

NikNote does that in 2 minutes. That is the whole pitch. It is not "AI-powered
learning platform". It is "your teachers stop wasting evenings copying
worksheets."

### Follow-up cadence

Most sales happen on message 3, not message 1.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | First message |
| 2 | "Sir, ek chhota demo bhej doon? 2 min ka video hai." (send a 60-sec screen recording) |
| 5 | Send one real example: their kind of worksheet, generated, as a PDF |
| 9 | "Koi pressure nahi — bas confirm kar raha hoon, interest hai ya nahi?" |
| 15 | Close the loop: "Main isse band kar raha hoon. Aage kabhi zaroorat ho to message kar dijiye." |
| 30 | Re-open only if something changed (new feature, new price) |

Never more than 5 messages without a reply. That is spam.

### Objection handling

**"Hum to already Teachmint/Classplus use karte hain."**
> Bilkul, woh administration ke liye hai — fees, attendance. NikNote uska
> replacement nahi hai. Yeh sirf worksheet banane ke liye hai. Dono saath
> chalte hain.

**"AI se banega to students ko pata chal jayega."**
> Handwriting real font se generate hoti hai, 16 styles, thoda natural
> variation ke saath. Aur honestly — aap isse worksheet aur answer key banane
> ke liye use kijiye, students ke assignment ke liye nahi. Teachers ka time
> bachana hi asli fayda hai.

**"Free mein to bahut apps hain."**
> Hain. Lekin unmein se zyadatar pe watermark aata hai ya page limit hai.
> Yahan 300 DPI print-ready PDF milta hai, offline chalta hai, aur ₹4,999 saal
> ka hai — mahine ke ₹417. Ek teacher ke 76 ghante ke saamne yeh kuch bhi nahi.

**"Sochke batata hoon."**
> Zaroor. Main 30 din ka free pilot abhi activate kar deta hoon — paisa baad
> mein, decision baad mein. 30 din baad khud bata dijiye kaam aaya ya nahi.

### Pricing to quote

| Plan | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Free pilot | ₹0 / 30 days | 1 department, up to 30 students |
| Teacher | ₹999 / year | 1 teacher login |
| Tuition Centre | ₹4,999 / year | 5 teacher logins |

**Do not discount the first price you quote.** If they push back, add value
(extra logins, onboarding call) instead of cutting price. A ₹4,999 deal that
took 3 discount rounds is worth less than a ₹4,999 deal that closed clean.

Benchmark for your own confidence: Indian coaching software runs
**₹6,000–₹20,000/year** (Justeach ₹5,999, Smart Classes ₹6,000, MyClassCampus
~₹15,000, Classtrix ₹13,800, Classplus ₹19,999/user). At ₹4,999 you are the
cheapest credible option, and your pitch is a different job-to-be-done anyway.

### Getting paid

You are not a registered business yet, most likely. Two paths:

1. **Under ₹20 lakh/year** — you can invoice as an individual/proprietorship.
   A school will usually still ask for a GSTIN. Many will pay anyway on a plain
   invoice with your PAN.
2. **Register for GST** once you cross ₹20 lakh turnover, or immediately if a
   school insists. It takes a few days and costs little.

Ask for **UPI or bank transfer** — not Razorpay checkout, which only supports
₹49/₹99 plans. Send a simple PDF invoice. Keep a copy of every one.

**Never take money for features you cannot deliver.** If they ask for a teacher
dashboard, say no and sell them what exists.

## Priority 2 — Consumers: fix the buying occasion (do this before spending on ads)

**The problem, stated plainly:**

LikhAI (likhai.in) sells text → handwriting at **₹5/page, pay-per-download, no
account**. Assignment King sells handwritten pages at **₹6/page**.

NikNote sells **₹49/week or ₹99/month, subscription only**.

A student with an assignment due tomorrow is an impulse buyer. They will pay
₹29 once in 30 seconds. They will not enter a recurring subscription for a
one-off job. NikNote is asking for commitment at the exact moment the customer
wants a one-time transaction.

**Recommended experiment (do not ship untested — see note below):**

Add a one-time "Download this PDF" option:
- ₹19 for 1 PDF, up to 20 pages
- ₹49 for a 10-PDF pack (no expiry)
- Keep the ₹99/month subscription as the upgrade path

Expected effect: converts impulse traffic that currently bounces at the
paywall. This is the single cheapest conversion test available.

**Why it is not shipped yet:** `verified_payments` has a database CHECK
constraint that only permits `('weekly','monthly')`, and
`activate_verified_purchase()` only accepts 4900/9900 paise. Changing money
code without being able to run a real Razorpay test end-to-end risks breaking
the one payment path that currently works. Do it carefully, with a single
₹1 test order.

## Priority 3 — Make traffic exist at all

Nothing above matters if nobody visits. In order of cheapness:

1. **Answer where the question is already asked.** Quora and Reddit threads on
   "text to handwriting converter", "assignment likhne ka tarika", "CBSE project
   file handwritten". Give a genuinely useful answer. Mention the tool only
   where it is actually the answer.
2. **YouTube Shorts.** Record your screen: paste a paragraph, pick a style,
   export. 20 seconds. The transformation is inherently watchable.
3. **SEO — but fix the plumbing first.** The site is a client-rendered SPA, so
   all 18 blog posts serve an identical `index.html`. Google cannot see
   distinct content per URL. Prerender the blog routes before writing post #19.
4. **Do not run Google Ads yet.** At ₹99/month with high student churn, you
   cannot afford a ₹40–80 click. Wait until you know real conversion.

## The tracker

`sales_leads` in Supabase is the system of record. Nothing lives in your head
or in WhatsApp.

```sql
-- Your daily work queue
select name, institution, phone, segment, stage, created_at
from sales_leads
where stage in ('new','contacted')
order by created_at;

-- Move a lead forward
update sales_leads
set stage = 'contacted', next_action_at = now() + interval '2 days'
where id = '<lead-id>';

-- The only number that matters
select stage, count(*) from sales_leads group by stage;
```

**Non-negotiable:** every conversation ends with a stage update and a
`next_action_at`. A pipeline you do not update is a list of phone numbers.

## Weekly targets to hold yourself to

| Activity | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New centres contacted | 25/week | 100/month is the minimum for 2–4 pilots |
| Demos delivered | 5/week | Demos close ~5x better than messages |
| Pilots started | 2/week | Free pilots are the only frictionless yes |
| Follow-ups sent | 15/week | Most revenue is on follow-up 3 |
| Pilot → paid conversion | 30% | If below 20%, the product is the problem, not the sales |

If after **100 centres contacted** you have **0 paid conversions**, stop selling
and go fix the product. That is information, not failure.
